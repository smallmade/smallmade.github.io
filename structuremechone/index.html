Why it is different from asking a chatbot

  • It is a family, not an answer. Change a number and watch the whole picture move. A single static answer cannot show you which way something is sensitive.
  • It shows you which relation produced each number. Every result names the relation beside it.
  • It says where it stops. Where a model is near the edge of its range, the app says so next to the number rather than after the fact.
  • It works with no network at all. On a plane, in an exam hall, in a basement lab.
  • Its workings are public. The theory manual lists every relation, every assumption and every limit.

How it was checked

Every relation is verified against sixty-digit reference values computed independently of the app, and the Apple and desktop implementations are compared against the same reference — 45 screens, both languages, one set of numbers.

Material data and reference values are taken from public-domain sources: Handbook of Structural Stability, Part I - Buckling of Flat Plates, Buckling Stresses of Simply Supported Rectangular Plates in Shear, Effective Width of Plates in Compression, A Unified Theory of Plastic Buckling of Columns and Plates, among others.
