Refunds

Last updated: 2026-08-19

Plot4Mac is sold through the App Store, and Apple handles refunds for everything bought there. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com with the Apple Account you bought it with; the decision and the money both come from Apple, not from us.

If something is wrong with the app

Write to softwareone.support@gmail.com either way. A refund request tells us nothing about what went wrong; a description of what you were plotting and what the app did instead is what gets it fixed.
