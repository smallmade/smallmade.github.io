What Plot4Mac is

Plot4Mac is a data visualization tool. It renders the numbers you give it — from a CSV file you import — as a line or contour plot, styled for journal submission. It does not perform any independent scientific, engineering, or statistical calculation, does not validate your input data, and does not check whether your data or your model is physically correct. It plots exactly what you give it.
