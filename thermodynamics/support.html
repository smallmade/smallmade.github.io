ThermalOne provides 54 engineering thermodynamics calculators for iPad and Mac, with formulas, assumptions, manuals and export tools available offline.

What it covers

AreaWhat it is for
State & propertiesFix a water/steam state from any two of pressure, temperature, quality, enthalpy or entropy. Ideal-gas properties from NASA polynomials. Real-gas behaviour from generalised compressibility charts and four cubic equations of state.
First lawProcess paths (polytropic, isothermal, isobaric, isochoric), closed-system energy balances, and cycle energy accounting — each solved for whichever quantity is missing.
Control-volume devicesNozzles, diffusers, turbines, compressors, pumps, heat exchangers, throttling devices, and transient filling/emptying of a vessel.
Second law & exergyReversible limits, entropy balances, isentropic efficiency, reversible steady-flow work, and a per-component exergy audit that ranks where a cycle destroys its own work potential.
Power & refrigeration cyclesRankine (with reheat and regeneration), Otto, Diesel, Dual, Brayton (with regeneration, reheat and intercooling), vapour-compression refrigeration, and heat pumps.
Moist airPsychrometric states, air-conditioning processes, and cooling-tower calculations.
Reacting systemsCombustion stoichiometry and products, reacting energy and entropy, flame temperature, chemical exergy, fuel cells, and equilibrium.
Design studiesLiquefaction, phase equilibrium, endoreversible power, thermal-radiation exergy, storage irreversibility, and entropy-generation minimisation.

Where the assumptions stop

Each calculator states the model it uses and presents invalid or out-of-range inputs directly on the screen. The in-app Theory Manual records the equations, assumptions and limits used by the current build.

ThermalOne is an educational calculation tool. It is not offered for flight safety, certification, or critical engineering decisions taken without independent review.

Getting help

Write to softwareone.support@gmail.com. Say which screen, what you entered, and what you expected. If a number looks wrong, include the exact inputs so it can be reproduced.

Reports that a result is wrong are taken seriously and checked against the reference sources rather than argued with.
