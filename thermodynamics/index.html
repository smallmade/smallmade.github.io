What it covers

State & properties

Water/steam from any two of p, T, x, h, s. Ideal-gas properties. Real-gas cubic equations of state.

First law

Process paths, closed-system energy balances, and cycle energy accounting, solved for the unknown term.

Control-volume devices

Nozzles, diffusers, turbines, compressors, pumps, heat exchangers, throttles, transient charging.

Second law & exergy

Entropy balances, isentropic efficiency, and a per-component exergy audit that ranks where work potential is destroyed.

Power cycles

Rankine with reheat and regeneration; Otto, Diesel, Dual; Brayton with regeneration, reheat and intercooling.

Refrigeration & heat pumps

Vapour-compression refrigeration and heat pump cycles.

Why it's not a printed table

  • Interactive. Drag an input and every output, and every point on the T-s or p-v diagram, updates in the same frame.
  • Verifiable. Every property routine is checked against IAPWS-IF97's own published verification tables and against NIST reference data — public sources, cited on screen.
  • Offline. No network connection of any kind. Works in a lab basement, an exam hall, or mid-flight.
  • Honest about limits. Where a result leaves a validated range, or differs from an older table's formulation, the app says so on screen instead of staying quiet.
