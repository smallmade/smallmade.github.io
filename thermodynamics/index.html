What it covers

State & properties

Water and steam states, ideal-gas properties and mixtures, real-gas cubic equations of state, departure functions and phase equilibrium.

First law

Process paths, closed-system energy balances, and cycle energy accounting, solved for the unknown term.

Control-volume devices

Nozzles, diffusers, turbines, compressors, pumps, heat exchangers, throttles, transient charging.

Second law & exergy

Entropy balances, isentropic efficiency, and a per-component exergy audit that ranks where work potential is destroyed.

Power cycles

Rankine, combined, Otto, Diesel, Dual, Brayton and turbojet cycles, including reheat, regeneration and intercooling.

Refrigeration & heat pumps

Vapour-compression, heat-pump, gas, cascade and multistage refrigeration, plus liquefaction.

Moist air

Psychrometric states, air-conditioning processes and cooling-tower calculations.

Reacting systems

Combustion, reacting energy and entropy, flame temperature, fuel cells and chemical equilibrium.
