Privacy Policy

Passthrough for macOS, iPadOS and iOS · Effective 21 August 2026 · 中文版

Passthrough does not collect any data. It has no network access whatsoever, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no crash reporting. Every video you convert is processed entirely on your own device and never leaves it.

What this app does

Passthrough converts video files to MP4 using the hardware video engine built into Apple Silicon. All processing happens locally on your device.

Data we collect

None. There is no category of personal or non-personal data that Passthrough collects, transmits, sells, or shares — not device identifiers, not usage statistics, not file names, not crash logs.

This is not a policy commitment we ask you to take on faith. On macOS the app ships without the network entitlement, so the sandbox refuses network access outright. On iPhone and iPad, where no such entitlement exists, the same guarantee holds structurally: the app contains no networking code, and the FFmpeg libraries it embeds were compiled with --disable-network. In both builds the ability to make a network connection is absent from the binary rather than merely unused.

Files you open

Passthrough reads only the video files you explicitly choose, and writes the converted result where you tell it to. It does not scan your disk, index your media library, or access files you have not selected. The app requests no permissions at all — it declares no usage descriptions, because it needs none.

On Mac, you supply files by dragging them in, using the Open dialog, or opening them from the Finder. The app runs in Apple's App Sandbox. When you ask it to save results next to the source file, macOS asks you once to grant access to that folder, because selecting an individual file does not grant write access to its parent directory.

On iPhone and iPad, you supply files through the system Files picker or the system photo picker. Both run outside the app, in their own process: the app never receives access to your Files storage or your photo library, only copies of the specific items you picked. This is why iOS never shows you a permission prompt for Passthrough — there is no access to grant. Access to a chosen file is held only while that file is being converted, and released immediately afterwards.

Data stored on your device

What is saved locally stays inside the app's own sandbox container and is never transmitted anywhere.

On Mac:

  • Your preferences — output location, codec, quality, audio and subtitle handling.
  • Folder permissions — macOS security-scoped bookmarks for folders you have authorised, so you are not asked again every time. You can erase these at any time from the app's settings, or by deleting the app.

On iPhone and iPad: only the converted videos themselves, written to the app's own Documents folder, which you can browse in the Files app under On My iPhone (or On My iPad) → Passthrough. No folder permissions are stored, because iOS has no equivalent mechanism and the app does not need one. Deleting the app removes everything it saved.

Third parties

Passthrough contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. It embeds FFmpeg (LGPL-2.1-or-later) purely as a local media processing library; FFmpeg does not collect data and, in this build, cannot access the network.

Children

Because no data is collected from anyone, no data is collected from children.

Your rights

Rights to access, correct, export, or delete personal data (under GDPR, CCPA and similar laws) apply to data a developer holds about you. We hold none, so there is nothing to request. Everything the app stores is on your own device and under your control.

Changes

If a future version of Passthrough ever adds a feature that involves a network connection, this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the change will be described here with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: softwareone.support@gmail.com

隐私政策

Passthrough for macOS、iPadOS 与 iOS · 生效日期 2026 年 8 月 21 日 · English

Passthrough 不收集任何数据。它完全不联网，没有账号、没有统计分析、 没有广告、没有崩溃上报。你转换的每一个视频都只在你自己的设备上处理，绝不离开这台设备。

这个 App 做什么

Passthrough 使用 Apple Silicon 内置的硬件视频引擎把视频转换为 MP4。 所有处理都在你的设备本地完成。

我们收集的数据

没有。Passthrough 不收集、不传输、不出售、不共享任何个人或非个人数据 —— 不包括设备标识符、使用统计、文件名，也不包括崩溃日志。

这不是一句要你凭信任接受的承诺。在 macOS 上，本 App 未申请网络权限， 沙盒会直接拒绝一切网络访问。在 iPhone 和 iPad 上并没有"网络权限"这种东西， 但同样的保证靠结构成立：App 里不含任何联网代码，内嵌的 FFmpeg 库在编译时 使用了 --disable-network。两个平台的构建都一样 ——"联网"这个能力 在二进制里根本不存在，而不是存在但没用。

你打开的文件

Passthrough 只读取你明确选择的视频文件，并把转换结果写到你指定的位置。 它不扫描你的磁盘、不建立媒体库索引，也不访问你没有选中的文件。本 App 不索取任何权限 —— 它没有声明任何权限用途说明，因为根本用不上。

在 Mac 上，你可以拖入窗口、通过"打开"对话框选择，或从访达中打开。 本 App 运行在 Apple 的 App Sandbox 中。当你选择把结果保存到源文件旁边时， macOS 会请你为该文件夹授权一次，因为选中单个文件并不等于获得其所在文件夹的写入权限。

在 iPhone 和 iPad 上，你通过系统的"文件"选择器或系统相片选择器提供文件。 这两个选择器都运行在 App 之外的独立进程里：App 拿不到你的文件存储或相册本身， 只会收到你选中的那几个项目的副本。这就是 iOS 从不为 Passthrough 弹出权限请求的原因 —— 没有需要授权的东西。对所选文件的访问只在转换期间持有，转换结束立即释放。

存在你设备上的数据

保存在本地的内容都在 App 自己的沙盒容器内，且永远不会被传输到任何地方。

在 Mac 上：

  • 你的偏好设置 —— 输出位置、编码、质量、音轨与字幕处理方式。
  • 文件夹授权 —— macOS 的 security-scoped bookmark， 记住你已授权的文件夹，免得每次都要重新授权。你可以随时在 App 设置中清除， 或直接删除 App。

在 iPhone 和 iPad 上：只有转换出来的视频本身，写在 App 自己的 Documents 目录里，你可以在"文件"App 的「我的 iPhone」（或「我的 iPad」）→ Passthrough 下浏览。不保存任何文件夹授权，因为 iOS 没有对应机制，App 也不需要。 删除 App 即可清除它保存的一切。

第三方

Passthrough 不含任何第三方统计、广告或追踪 SDK。 它内嵌 FFmpeg（LGPL-2.1-or-later）仅作为本地媒体处理库使用； FFmpeg 本身不收集数据，且在本构建中无法访问网络。

儿童隐私

由于不从任何人收集数据，自然也不从儿童收集数据。

你的权利

GDPR、CCPA 等法律赋予的访问、更正、导出、删除个人数据的权利， 针对的是开发者持有的关于你的数据。我们不持有任何此类数据，因此没有可供申请的对象。 App 保存的一切都在你自己的设备上，由你完全掌控。

政策变更

如果未来某个版本的 Passthrough 加入了涉及网络连接的功能， 本政策会在该版本发布前更新，并在此处说明变更内容和新的生效日期。
